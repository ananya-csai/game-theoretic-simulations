==============================================================================
  MODEL 1 — The advertising war (2 firms, Low vs High budget)
==============================================================================
  Market value M0 = ₹100 lakh, Low = ₹10 lakh, High = ₹30 lakh, share = s_i/(s_i+s_j)

  Firm A \ Firm B | Low        | High      
  ----------------+------------+-----------
  Low             | 40.0, 40.0 | 15.0, 45.0
  High            | 45.0, 15.0 | 20.0, 20.0

  Pure Nash equilibrium : ['(High, High)']  -> profits 20, 20
  Dominant strategy     : 'High' for both firms
  Pareto-optimal cells  : ['(Low, Low)', '(Low, High)', '(High, Low)']
  Game type             : Prisoner's Dilemma  (T=45 > R=40 > P=20 > S=15)
  IESDS                 : Firm A: 'Low' is strictly dominated by 'High'
  IESDS                 : Firm B: 'Low' is strictly dominated by 'High'
  Joint profit lost at equilibrium: ₹40 lakh (50% of the cooperative outcome)

  Three budget levels (10 / 25 / 40):

  Firm A \ Firm B | Low (10)   | Medium (25) | High (40) 
  ----------------+------------+-------------+-----------
  Low (10)        | 40.0, 40.0 | 18.6, 46.4  | 10.0, 40.0
  Medium (25)     | 46.4, 18.6 | 25.0, 25.0  | 13.5, 21.5
  High (40)       | 40.0, 10.0 | 21.5, 13.5  | 10.0, 10.0

  Nash equilibrium: ['(Medium (25), Medium (25))'] — the same ₹25 lakh each that the continuous model predicts.
  Mixed-strategy search (support enumeration) finds 1 equilibrium in total -> it is unique.

  Sensitivity (High budget 11–80, market growth 0–2):
     Deadlock (High spend efficient)     49.4% of scenarios
     Prisoner's Dilemma                  41.4% of scenarios
     Harmony (Low spend dominant)         8.0% of scenarios
     Boundary (ties)                      1.2% of scenarios

==============================================================================
  MODEL 3 — Keyword (sponsored search) auctions: GSP vs VCG vs GFP
==============================================================================
  Values per click (₹): Brand A=12, Brand B=10, Brand C=8, Brand D=6, Brand E=4
  Click-through rates : slot 1 = 30%, slot 2 = 20%, slot 3 = 10%

  Mechanism              | Bids              | Slot winners              | Price/click (slots 1-3) | Revenue | Welfare
  -----------------------+-------------------+---------------------------+-------------------------+---------+--------
  VCG (truthful)         | [12, 10, 8, 6, 4] | Brand A, Brand B, Brand C | 8.0, 7.0, 6.0           | 4.40    | 6.40   
  GSP (truthful bids)    | [12, 10, 8, 6, 4] | Brand A, Brand B, Brand C | 10.0, 8.0, 6.0          | 5.20    | 6.40   
  GSP (equilibrium bids) | [12, 8, 7, 6, 4]  | Brand A, Brand B, Brand C | 8.0, 7.0, 6.0           | 4.40    | 6.40   
  GFP (truthful bids)    | [12, 10, 8, 6, 4] | Brand A, Brand B, Brand C | 12.0, 10.0, 8.0         | 6.40    | 6.40   

  Truthful bidding an equilibrium under VCG?  True
  Truthful bidding an equilibrium under GSP?  False
     -> Brand A earns 0.60 bidding 12, but 0.80 by bidding ≈8 and dropping to slot 2.
  EOS equilibrium bids [12.0, 8.0, 7.0, 6.0, 4.0] form a Nash equilibrium of GSP? True
  Under GFP truthful bids leave every advertiser with zero profit, so they always shade -> no stable bids (Model 4).
  EOS textbook example (values 10/4/2, clicks 200/199): truthful profit 1200 vs 1592 after shading to ≈2

  Monte Carlo: 10,000 auctions, 5 bidders, values ~ U[0, 10]

  Mechanism              | Avg revenue | Std dev | Avg welfare | Revenue / welfare
  -----------------------+-------------+---------+-------------+------------------
  VCG (truthful)         | 2.672       | 0.982   | 4.327       | 61.7%            
  GSP (truthful bids)    | 3.333       | 0.981   | 4.327       | 77.0%            
  GSP (equilibrium bids) | 2.672       | 0.982   | 4.327       | 61.7%            

==============================================================================
  MODEL 4 — Bidding dynamics: why the industry moved from GFP to GSP
==============================================================================
  GFP (2 advertisers, values 10 and 8): bids climb to ₹3.4 then crash to the ₹0.5 reserve.
  Number of crashes in 400 revisions: 13  -> bids never settle (no pure-strategy equilibrium)
  GSP balanced bidding: 20/20 random starting points converge to the EOS equilibrium bids
  Median updates to converge: 30; final revenue = ₹4.40 = VCG revenue ₹4.40
